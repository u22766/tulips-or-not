How to play

Everyone hides a five-letter word. Dice set the turn order, then play goes one at a time on a timer.

On your turn you secretly pick a target and guess a word against them. Everyone sees your guess and the feedback — but only you know whose word it was scored against. Stay paranoid.

2:1 means two letters right-spot and one elsewhere — in somebody's word.

Name someone's word exactly and they're knocked out (their word is revealed). Run out the clock and your turn is skipped — three skips in a row and the game assumes you've wandered off and knocks you out too.

Last player standing wins.
