The "Is this price fair?" checklist

1Earnings & growth. What does it actually earn — and is that growing, flat, or shrinking?
2Durability. Can rivals easily copy it, or does a moat protect its profits?
3The price. What multiple are you paying? A steady business is worth ~15×; a grower can justify roughly its growth rate. Is the number justified?
4What could go wrong? If it looks cheap, ask why — a low price on a shrinking business is a trap, not a bargain.

Do it for real: use a screener

→Most brokerages (and free stock screeners) let you filter companies by P/E, growth, and sector.
→Filter for what you learned: a fair multiple for the growth, real earnings, a durable business.
→The screener finds names — you judge them with the checklist. That's the whole skill.
This game teaches a way of thinking, not what to buy. It isn't financial advice — real decisions and real risks are yours. Prices and multiples change constantly, so always check current numbers before deciding anything.
