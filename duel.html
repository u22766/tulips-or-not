How to play

Each side hides a five-letter word. A coin toss decides who goes first, then you take turns guessing at each other's word. After every guess you get back two numbers.

2:1 means two letters are correct and in the right spot, and one more letter is in the word but sitting somewhere else.

Repeated letters count only as often as they actually appear. If the hidden word is SPEED and you guess GEESE, you get 1:2.

Prefer an easier ride? Switch clues to Colors in setup: every guessed letter is painted — teal for right spot, outlined for in-the-word — so there's nothing to compute, just like the solo game.

Guesses and clues are public — both of you can see everything on the board. Only the secret words are hidden. First to name the other's word wins.
