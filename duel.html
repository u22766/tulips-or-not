How to play

You and your opponent each hide a five-letter word. A coin toss decides who goes first, then you take turns guessing at each other's word. After every guess you get back two numbers.

2:1 means two letters are correct and in the right spot, and one more letter is in the word but sitting somewhere else.

Repeated letters count only as often as they actually appear. If the hidden word is SPEED and you guess GEESE, you get 1:2 — one E lands in the right spot, and only two of your other letters can be matched against the remaining letters.

First one to name the other's word wins, full stop. Going first is a real edge, which is exactly why the coin decides it.
